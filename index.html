About Me

Who Am I

Hi, I'm Ines Quezada, a bilingual UGC creator and professional makeup artist with 15+ years of experience in the beauty industry.

As a co-founder of The Makeup Shack, a professional makeup brand, I've spent years working closely with beauty products and the people who use them. I bring that hands-on knowledge to the content I create, from makeup and skincare demonstrations to relatable lifestyle videos.

I also have 300+ hours of live selling experience in English and Spanish, connecting with viewers and explaining products on camera.

What I Do

I create short-form videos, product demonstrations, tutorials, and lifestyle content for brands. My content focuses on beauty, makeup, skincare, hair, fashion, home, and pet accessories — available in English and Spanish.

My Why

Why I Create Content?

I create content because I love helping people discover products they can actually use and enjoy. From a makeup routine to a home find, I like showing the details that help someone picture a product in their own life.

My favorite content feels natural, useful, and true to the way I speak and share with my audience in English or Spanish.

Process

From Idea To Final Cut

I start by learning about your product, audience, and the message you want to share. Then I plan a concept that feels natural to my style and fits the platform where it will appear.

From filming the product in use to editing the final video, I focus on clear visuals, an engaging opening, and details that help viewers understand the product. Content can be created in English or Spanish.
